CASH FLOW FORECASTING

Monitor Income And Expenses With Confidence

A professional cash flow forecasting service gives restaurant owners and managers a clear view of where their money is coming from, where it is going, and what lies ahead.

What We Provide

  • 12-month cash flow forecasts – Project income, expenses and cash balances.
  • Weekly & monthly cash flow monitoring – Track actual performance against forecasts.
  • Income forecasting – Analyse sales trends, seasonality, events and expected revenue.
  • Expense forecasting – Monitor food, beverage, payroll, rent, utilities and operating costs.
  • Cash-flow gap identification – Highlight potential shortfalls before they become problems.

Planning & Reporting

  • Supplier & payment planning – Schedule payments to protect available cash.
  • Scenario planning – Model best-case, expected and worst-case situations.
  • Management reporting – Simple dashboards showing the financial position at a glance.
  • Corrective action planning – Identify areas where costs need to be controlled or revenue improved.
THE RESULT: Know your cash position before it becomes a problem.
FOOD COST ANALYSIS

Reduce Waste And Improve Profit Margins

Accurate food cost analysis helps restaurants understand exactly where money is being spent and where profit is being lost.

Our Food Cost Analysis Services

  • Recipe costing – Calculate the exact cost of every ingredient in each dish.
  • Menu engineering – Identify your most profitable and least profitable menu items.
  • Portion control – Standardise portions to reduce over-serving and unnecessary food usage.
  • Waste analysis – Track preparation waste, spoilage, returns and overproduction.
  • Supplier & purchasing review – Identify opportunities to improve pricing and purchasing decisions.

Profit Protection

  • Food cost percentage – Monitor actual food costs against your target percentage.
  • Menu pricing – Ensure selling prices provide the required gross profit margin.
  • Stock control – Improve inventory management and reduce stock losses.
  • Profitability analysis – Turn food-cost data into practical actions that improve the bottom line.
THE GOAL: Less waste, better purchasing, consistent portions and stronger restaurant profitability.

4. Practical Training Method

70% – Practical: Staff learn through hands-on work and real operational situations.

20% – Coaching: Supervisors and managers observe employees, provide feedback and demonstrate correct procedures.

10% – Theory: Short classroom or online sessions covering policies, procedures, food safety, costing, customer service and management principles.
